Privacy Policy — Reels Studio

Reels Studio requests only the permissions needed to upload a video to the authorized account's drafts: basic profile info (to show which account is connected) and video upload.

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private server and are used only to upload the owner's own videos. No data from TikTok is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data about other TikTok users is collected.

To remove access, revoke the app in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions); stored tokens for that account are then deleted.
