Terms of Service — Reels Studio

Reels Studio is a private tool used by its owner to prepare short videos for the owner's own TikTok accounts. The tool uploads a finished video to the TikTok inbox (drafts) of an account that the owner has authorized. The video is then reviewed, edited and published manually by the account owner inside the TikTok app.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only accounts that belong to the owner are connected. Access to an account can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

The tool does not post anything publicly on its own and does not interact with other users' content.
